Incentive compensation on Kennect

Sales incentive calculations for a pharmaceutical client: targets, slabs and payouts.

My role
Associate Consultant
Period
Jan to Sep 2026
Platform
Kennect, SQL, JavaScript rules
Status
Client work

The problem

Commission payouts across several sales hierarchies were calculated by hand, which made them slow to reconcile and easy to get wrong.

My part

  • Data pipelines from sales transactions to incentive calculations
  • JavaScript business rules, workflows and validation
  • SQL logic for targets, achievement slabs and payouts
  • Anaplan data fixes in a parallel workstream

How a payout is calculated

  1. Sales dataTransactions by territory
  2. ValidationRules catch bad records
  3. TargetsAllocated down the hierarchy
  4. SlabsAchievement mapped to a rate
  5. PayoutAudit‑ready report

Conceptual flow. Client rules and data are not shown.

Slabs, in plain terms

A slab table maps bands of target achievement to payout rates. The rules apply it the same way for every rep, every period.

Outcome

Fewer manual payout errors, clearer reconciliation and reporting that finance and HR can audit.
