All work

Enterprise FP&A on Anaplan

Budgeting, forecasting and P&L reporting for a global publishing client.

My role
Associate Consultant, model building and testing
Period
May 2025 to Jan 2026
Scale
20+ business units, 35+ cost centres
Platform
Anaplan, with SAP actuals

The problem

Finance needed one view of revenue, OPEX and P&L, planned against actuals, for every business unit and cost centre.

My part

  • Revenue, OPEX and P&L modules, allocations and scenarios
  • 50+ line‑item calculations, from allocations to YoY variance
  • DCA, ALM revisions and PLANS standards
  • UAT cycles, validation and release support

How the work ran

  1. RequirementsWith finance stakeholders
  2. Model designLists, hierarchies, modules
  3. Planning logicDrivers, allocations, versions
  4. TestingUAT and calculation checks
  5. ReportingBudget and forecast against actuals

Conceptual flow. Client screens are not shown.

Driver‑based planning

Planners set a driver, and the model spreads cost by it instead of hand‑typed figures. Change the driver and every allocation follows.

Scenarios

Actual, budget and forecast sit side by side as versions, so variance is a calculation, not a spreadsheet.

Outcome

Faster model calculations, fewer manual planning errors and less effort to produce reports.
