Business management for small shops: customers, products, orders, invoices and payments. Live as INVi.

The problem

Many shopkeepers run without billing software, and some have no laptop. The product has to stay simple enough for any kind of shop.

Version 1 scope

  • Users, customers and products, with CSV import
  • Orders, one invoice each, stored with the order
  • Payments and refunds on a ledger
  • A dashboard of payments by date, and dark mode

How a sale moves

  1. Set upCustomers and products, or a CSV
  2. OrderItems, quantities, totals
  3. InvoiceOne per order, snapshotted
  4. PaymentRecorded on the ledger
  5. DashboardPayments by date

The planned version 1 flow.

Handling money safely

Retries are idempotent, so the same payment is never recorded twice. Invoices keep a snapshot, so later price changes do not rewrite them.

Next

Version 2: hourly analytics and campaigns that turn quiet hours into sales. WhatsApp notifications are being explored.
